Simulated Decision Heuristics & Rules

Complete, transparent documentation of the simulation rules governing this prototype:

🎯 25% Moisture Target

Soil readings at or above 25% are marked optimal. These zones request 0 L, sparing water from being wasted on saturated roots.

🚨 Critical Deficit (< 20%)

Requests a simulated dose of up to 20 L. Placed at top priority to rescue dehydrated plant root systems immediately.

⚠️ Moderate Deficit (20% to < 25%)

Requests a simulated dose of up to 10 L. Restores soil back to a safe buffer threshold.

🔍 Missing Sensor Protection

Blank readings allocate 0 L and explicitly flag "Check this zone before watering". Protects against irrigation line breaks or blind watering.

🏆 Priority Sorting & Tie-Breakers

Water is allocated to eligible zones from lowest moisture to highest. Equal readings break ties in alphabetical order (A → B → C). Partial doses are shown when the budget runs out.

⚖️ Usual Schedule Baseline

Divides budget equally across all 3 zones (Math.floor(budget / 3)) with whole-litre remainders distributed in A, then B order.
