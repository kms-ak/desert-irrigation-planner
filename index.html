How this was calculated ▾

Simulated target: 25% soil moisture threshold.

Dose heuristics: Readings < 20% request up to 20 L; readings 20% to < 25% request up to 10 L; readings ≥ 25% receive 0 L.

Priority order: Allocated to eligible deficit zones from lowest moisture to highest. Ties broken in A, B, C order. Water is capped by remaining budget.

Usual benchmark: Splits budget equally across all 3 zones with whole-litre remainders assigned in A, B, C order.

Deployment in a real production greenhouse would require locally calibrated soil moisture thresholds, soil water retention curves, and crop-specific dosage models.

💧 Targeted Water Allocation

Under the simulated rules, water is directed to the lowest-reading zones first. Moist zones (≥25%) receive 0 L, sparing water when the daily supply is limited.

⚖️ Equal-Split vs. Priority Tradeoff

Under an equal split, each zone gets an identical fraction regardless of sensor readings. Under the recommended plan, available litres are concentrated where moisture deficits are greatest.

Simulated Decision Heuristics & Calibration Notes

Prioritizing zones under simulated decision heuristics:

🎯 25% Moisture Target (Simulated)

Soil readings at or above 25% are marked above target. These zones request 0 L, withholding water when moisture is already adequate.

🚨 Critical Deficit (< 20%)

Requests a simulated dose of up to 20 L. Prioritized first for watering before the daily water budget is exhausted.

⚠️ Moderate Deficit (20% to < 25%)

Requests a simulated dose of up to 10 L to bring soil up to the simulated target threshold.

🔍 Missing Sensor Reading

Blank readings allocate 0 L and explicitly flag "Check reading", avoiding unverified water delivery until the sensor is confirmed.

🏆 Priority Sorting & Tie-Breakers

Allocates to eligible deficit zones from lowest moisture to highest. Ties break in alphabetical order (A → B → C). Partial doses are shown when budget is depleted.

🔬 Real-World Calibration Requirement

Deployment in a production greenhouse would require locally calibrated soil moisture thresholds, soil water retention curves, and crop-specific dosage models.
