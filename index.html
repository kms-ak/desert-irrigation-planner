Simulated Decision Rules Reference

Clear, verifiable heuristics driving this hackathon simulation prototype:

🎯 25% Moisture Target

Soil at or above 25% is marked optimal. 0 L is allocated to prevent overwatering, root rot, and evaporation.

🚨 Critical Deficit (< 20%)

Requests simulated dose of up to 20 L. Prioritized 1st to preserve dehydrated root zones.

⚠️ Moderate Deficit (20% to < 25%)

Requests simulated dose of up to 10 L. Brings soil back to safe buffer capacity.

🔍 Missing / Offline Sensors

Allocates 0 L and flags "Check this zone before watering" to prevent blind water application.

🏆 Priority Sorting & Tie-Breakers

Allocates from driest to wettest. Ties broken in A, B, C order. Shows partial allocation if budget runs out.

⚖️ Usual Schedule Baseline

Even division across all 3 zones with whole-litre remainder distributed in A, B, C order.
