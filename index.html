📐 Simulated Decision Rules Reference

Target Moisture: 25% Soil ≥ 25% is marked optimal and gets 0 L to avoid overwatering and evaporation.
Critical Deficit (< 20%) Requests simulated dose of up to 20 L. Prioritized 1st for root relief.
Moderate Deficit (20% to < 25%) Requests simulated dose of up to 10 L to restore buffer moisture.
Missing Sensor (Blank) Recommends 0 L and flags "Check this zone before watering" for safety.
Priority & Tie-Breakers Driest zone watered first. Ties broken in A, B, C order. Shows partial allocation.
Usual Baseline Even split across all 3 zones with whole-litre remainders to A, then B.
