Simulated Decision Logic Reference

These heuristics simulate decision-support logic for small desert greenhouse systems:

Target Moisture: 25% Zones ≥ 25% are marked optimal and receive 0 L to prevent unnecessary watering.
Dose Tier 1: Severe Deficit (< 20%) Requests simulated dose of up to 20 L. Prioritized first for immediate root relief.
Dose Tier 2: Moderate Deficit (20% to < 25%) Requests simulated dose of up to 10 L to bring soil up to target buffer.
Blank / Missing Sensors Allocates 0 L and flags "Check this zone before watering" to avoid blind irrigation.
Priority Allocation & Tie-Breaker Driest zone receives water first. Ties break in A, B, C order. Water is capped by remaining budget.
Usual Schedule Benchmark Even division across all 3 zones (Math.floor(budget / 3) with remainders to A, then B).
