Ration Scarce Water Where Desert Crops Need It Most

When daily greenhouse irrigation is severely restricted, traditional equal-split routines waste litres on already-moist soil. Desert Irrigation Planner prioritizes water strictly to critical root deficits before supply runs dry.

Deficit-First Prioritization

Zones below the 25% target moisture threshold receive water in ascending order (driest zone first). Crops under high drought stress are stabilized before water runs out.

Sensor Fault Protection

Blank or missing sensor readings allocate 0 L and explicitly flag "Check this zone before watering", stopping blind irrigation of unverified soil lines.

💧 Smart Water Redirection Benefit

Instead of indiscriminately pouring equal fractions into every zone, the priority planner identifies zones with soil moisture ≥ 25% (Target) and prevents wasteful water application.

🛡️ Moisture Target Buffer

Zones between 20% and 24% are topped up with a moderate 10 L dose, while severe drought zones (<20%) receive up to 20 L to restore root vitality.

Simulated Decision Heuristics & Rules

Complete, transparent documentation of the simulation rules governing this prototype:

🎯 25% Moisture Target

Soil readings at or above 25% are marked optimal. These zones request 0 L, sparing water from being wasted on saturated roots.

🚨 Critical Deficit (< 20%)

Requests a simulated dose of up to 20 L. Placed at top priority to rescue dehydrated plant root systems immediately.

⚠️ Moderate Deficit (20% to < 25%)

Requests a simulated dose of up to 10 L. Restores soil back to a safe buffer threshold.

🔍 Missing Sensor Protection

Blank readings allocate 0 L and explicitly flag "Check this zone before watering". Protects against irrigation line breaks or blind watering.

🏆 Priority Sorting & Tie-Breakers

Water is allocated to eligible zones from lowest moisture to highest. Equal readings break ties in alphabetical order (A → B → C). Partial doses are shown when the budget runs out.

⚖️ Usual Schedule Baseline

Divides budget equally across all 3 zones (Math.floor(budget / 3)) with whole-litre remainders distributed in A, then B order.
