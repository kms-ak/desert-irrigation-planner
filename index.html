📐 Transparent Simulation Rules

Operational heuristics governing this desert irrigation simulation prototype:

Target Moisture: 25%

Soil at or above 25% is marked optimal. Allocates 0 L to prevent waterlogging and evaporative loss.

Critical Deficit (< 20%)

Requests up to 20 L. Placed at top priority to rescue dehydrated plant root systems.

Moderate Deficit (20% to < 25%)

Requests up to 10 L. Restores soil moisture to optimal buffer capacity.

Offline / Blank Sensors

Recommends 0 L and flags "Check this zone before watering" to avoid blind irrigation.

Budget Allocation & Tie-Breakers

Allocates water to driest zones first. Ties break in A, B, C order. Shows partial doses if budget runs out.

Usual Schedule Baseline

Even division across all 3 zones with whole-litre remainders assigned to A, then B.
